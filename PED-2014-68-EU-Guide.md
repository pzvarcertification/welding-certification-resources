# PED 2014/68/EU Certification – Pressure Equipment Compliance Guide

The **Pressure Equipment Directive (PED) 2014/68/EU** establishes requirements for applicable pressure equipment and assemblies placed on the European Union market. Manufacturers supplying pressure equipment to Europe need to determine whether their products fall within the scope of the directive and identify the appropriate conformity assessment requirements.

For manufacturers of pressure vessels, piping, boilers, heat exchangers and other pressure equipment, understanding PED classification and conformity assessment is an important part of preparing products for the European market.

## What Is PED 2014/68/EU?

PED 2014/68/EU applies to applicable pressure equipment and assemblies with a maximum allowable pressure greater than **0.5 bar**, subject to the scope and exclusions defined by the directive.

The directive covers different categories of pressure equipment, including:

* Pressure vessels
* Piping
* Boilers
* Heat exchangers
* Pressure accessories
* Safety accessories
* Assemblies

The applicable requirements are not the same for every product. Classification can depend on factors such as the maximum allowable pressure, volume, nominal size, fluid group and type of equipment.

Manufacturers should therefore establish the applicable PED category and conformity assessment route based on the characteristics of the equipment.

## PED Equipment Classification

One of the important stages in PED compliance is determining the equipment category.

Depending on the equipment type and its characteristics, pressure equipment can fall into different categories under the directive. The classification influences the conformity assessment procedure that must be followed.

Manufacturers may need to evaluate:

* Maximum allowable pressure
* Equipment volume or nominal size
* Fluid characteristics
* Fluid group
* Equipment type
* Applicable PED category
* Conformity assessment module

Correct classification is important because it determines the level and type of conformity assessment applicable to the equipment.

## PED Conformity Assessment

After determining the applicable classification, the manufacturer needs to identify the appropriate conformity assessment procedure.

Depending on the equipment category, the conformity assessment may involve requirements related to:

* Technical documentation
* Design and manufacturing controls
* Materials
* Manufacturing processes
* Welding and joining
* Inspection and testing
* Quality requirements
* Final assessment
* CE marking
* Declaration of conformity

The manufacturer should maintain the required technical documentation and demonstrate that the equipment satisfies the applicable essential safety requirements of PED 2014/68/EU.

## Welding Requirements Under PED

Welding is an important manufacturing activity for many types of pressure equipment. Welded pressure vessels, piping systems, boilers and related components require controlled welding processes and qualified personnel where applicable.

Depending on the equipment, manufacturing route and applicable PED requirements, manufacturers may need appropriate:

* Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS)
* Welding Procedure Qualification Records (WPQR)
* Welder qualifications
* Welding quality documentation
* Inspection and testing records
* Material documentation

**EN ISO 3834** may also be relevant where welding quality requirements apply to the manufacturing activities.

For manufacturers involved in pressure equipment production, welding documentation should be consistent with the applicable technical and regulatory requirements.

## PED and EN ISO 3834

**EN ISO 3834** specifies quality requirements for fusion welding of metallic materials. It can be relevant to manufacturers whose production activities involve welded pressure equipment and other fabricated products.

The relationship between PED requirements and welding quality should be considered during the manufacturing and conformity assessment process.

## Why PED Certification Matters for Manufacturers

For manufacturers exporting applicable pressure equipment to Europe, understanding PED requirements at an early stage can reduce uncertainty during product compliance activities.

A structured approach can help manufacturers establish:

* Whether the product falls within PED scope
* The applicable equipment classification
* The conformity assessment procedure
* Required technical documentation
* Relevant manufacturing requirements
* Welding qualification requirements
* Inspection and testing requirements
* CE marking and conformity documentation

The exact requirements depend on the equipment and its classification, so manufacturers should assess their individual products against the applicable provisions of PED 2014/68/EU.
