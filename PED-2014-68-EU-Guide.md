# PED 2014/68/EU Certification – Pressure Equipment Guide

The Pressure Equipment Directive (PED) 2014/68/EU establishes requirements for applicable pressure equipment and assemblies placed on the European market.

For manufacturers exporting pressure equipment to Europe, determining the applicable PED scope, classification and conformity assessment procedure is an important part of product compliance.

## What Does PED 2014/68/EU Cover?

PED 2014/68/EU applies to applicable pressure equipment and assemblies with a maximum allowable pressure greater than 0.5 bar, subject to the scope and exclusions of the directive.

Depending on the product, manufacturers may need to consider:

- Pressure vessels
- Piping
- Boilers
- Heat exchangers
- Pressure accessories
- Safety accessories
- Assemblies

The applicable requirements depend on factors such as pressure, volume or nominal size, fluid characteristics and equipment classification.

## PED Conformity Assessment

Manufacturers need to determine the appropriate conformity assessment route for their equipment.

Important considerations include:

- PED scope
- Equipment classification
- Conformity assessment procedure
- Technical documentation
- Materials
- Manufacturing requirements
- Welding and joining processes
- Inspection and testing
- CE marking

The applicable requirements can vary according to the equipment and its classification.

## Welding Requirements

Welding can form an important part of pressure equipment manufacturing.

Depending on the applicable requirements, manufacturers may need appropriate welding procedures, procedure qualifications, welder qualifications and relevant quality documentation.

Related welding standards and certification areas may include:

- EN ISO 3834
- WPS
- WPQR
- Welder Certification
- Welding quality requirements
