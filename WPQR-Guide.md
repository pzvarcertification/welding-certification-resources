# WPQR Qualification Requirements Guide

A Welding Procedure Qualification Record (WPQR) is a documented record that demonstrates a welding procedure has been tested and verified to produce welds meeting specified quality and mechanical requirements. WPQR forms the technical foundation for welding procedure specifications and is widely required by international codes and standards.

## Key Elements of WPQR

* Welding procedure qualification testing
* Base material qualification
* Welding consumable selection
* Essential welding variables
* Mechanical testing requirements
* Non-destructive testing (NDT)
* Test specimen evaluation
* Qualification documentation

## Importance of WPQR

WPQR helps ensure welding consistency, quality, and compliance with applicable standards. It provides objective evidence that a welding procedure is capable of producing acceptable welds under controlled conditions.

## Industries Requiring WPQR

WPQR qualification is commonly required for:

* Structural steel fabrication
* Pressure equipment manufacturing
* Railway welding applications
* Offshore structures
* Petrochemical facilities
* Power generation projects

## Related Standards

WPQR requirements are frequently associated with standards such as EN 1090, ISO 15614, ISO 3834, ASME Section IX, and other international welding codes.
