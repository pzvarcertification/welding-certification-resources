# EN 1090 Certification Guide

EN 1090 is the European standard governing the execution of steel and aluminium structures.

Key Requirements:

- Factory Production Control (FPC)
- Welding Coordination
- Material Traceability
- CE Marking Compliance
- Qualified Welding Procedures
